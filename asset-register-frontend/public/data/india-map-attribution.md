# India state boundaries

State and union territory boundaries were derived from the DataMeet India community map dataset, `States/Admin2`.

- Source: [DataMeet maps — States/Admin2](https://github.com/datameet/maps/tree/master/States)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: converted from the supplied WGS 84 shapefile to GeoJSON and simplified for browser display.

These outlines support navigation and roll-up counts. They are not cadastral, legal, or survey boundaries. Centre markers use only coordinates saved in the register and marked centre-head-confirmed or verified; no address is geocoded or guessed by the app.
